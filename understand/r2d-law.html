The R²D Law

One recursive relation, read from two adjacent boundaries

The preceding architecture has separated several things that conventional physical descriptions often combine.

At a local boundary \(B_n\), we can define:

  • a readable closure;
  • enclosing-defined possibility;
  • local retention;
  • net closure asymmetry;
  • and the number of times that closure recurs.

At the enclosing boundary \(B_{n+1}\), the same recursively organized realization has a different readable form:

  • an enclosing occurrence;
  • an enclosing distinguishable interval;
  • an enclosing constraint;
  • and its own recurrence count.

The R2D law proposes that these two boundary-specific readings are not independent.

They are two count expressions of one recursive realization.

The law is

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

Using

\[ \Delta A = \Delta P-\Delta R, \]

the same law can be written

\[ \boxed{ \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} - \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

This is the R2D law.

One Relation, Two Readings

Consider a completed representative local closure

\[ \mathcal C_{n,\alpha}. \]

At the enclosing boundary, the same recursively organized relation is read through an enclosing occurrence

\[ \chi_{n+1}^{(r)}. \]

These are not the same boundary-defined object:

\[ \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \not\equiv \chi_{n+1}^{(r)}. \]

The local boundary reads a completed closure.

The enclosing boundary reads an enclosing occurrence.

Recursive carry relates their count meanings:

\[ \boxed{ \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \xmapsto{\kappa_n} \chi_{n+1}^{(r)}. } \]

But this arrow is reclassification, not causation.

Recursive Carry →

The Causal Relation Still Points Downward

The enclosing occurrence defines which local realizations are compatible with it.

Thus the causal relation remains

\[ \boxed{ \chi_{n+1}^{(r)} \Longrightarrow \mathcal C_{n,\alpha}. } \]

The local closure does not independently generate the enclosing occurrence.

This distinction must remain intact when reading the R2D law.

The equation

\[ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha} \]

is not a causal arrow from left to right.

Nor is it a causal arrow from right to left.

It is an invariant recursive relation between two boundary-specific readings.

The Causal Inversion →

The Local Reading

At the representative peer boundary \(B_{n,\alpha}\), the completed closure has accumulated possibility

\[ \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}, \]

accumulated retention

\[ \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}, \]

and therefore net closure asymmetry

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} - \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}. } \]

This is the directional asymmetry remaining around one completed local closure.

Closure →

Asymmetry →

The Local Recurrence Count

Now choose one recurrence-comparison domain

\[ \mathcal D_R. \]

Let

\[ N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right) \]

be the number of completed recurrences of the local closure within that domain.

The recurrence-weighted local asymmetry is therefore

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right). } \]

This quantity is still dimensionless.

It is not:

  • energy;
  • power;
  • elapsed time;
  • force;
  • or physical work.

It is simply the asymmetry associated with one completed local closure, weighted by the number of times that closure recurs in the chosen comparison domain.

The Enclosing Reading

At boundary \(B_{n+1}\), the same recursively organized relation is read differently.

Let

\[ \lambda_{n+1} \]

be the enclosing distinguishable interval.

Let

\[ C_{n+1} \]

be the total enclosing constraint.

The constrained distinguishability associated with one enclosing recurrence is

\[ \boxed{ C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}. } \]

Again, neither quantity is yet assigned a specific physical unit.

The product is a dimensionless constrained-distinguishability relation.

The Enclosing Recurrence Count

Within the same recurrence-comparison domain

\[ \mathcal D_R, \]

let

\[ N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \chi_{n+1} \right) \]

count completed recurrences of the enclosing transition class.

The recurrence-weighted enclosing constrained distinguishability is

\[ \boxed{ C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \chi_{n+1} \right). } \]

The two boundaries therefore provide two complete count expressions of the same recursively organized relation.

The Recurrence-Weighted Form

R2D postulates that these two recurrence-weighted readings are equal:

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right) = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \chi_{n+1} \right). } \]

This is the most explicit form of the R2D law.

It says that over one common recurrence-comparison domain,

local recurrence-weighted closure asymmetry

and

enclosing recurrence-weighted constrained distinguishability

are two equivalent readings of one recursive relation.

Introduce the Recurrence Ratio

The adjacent recurrence ratio is

\[ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha} = \frac{ N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \chi_{n+1} \right) }{ N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right) }. \]

This asks how many enclosing recurrences occur per completed recurrence of the representative local closure.

Recurrence →

Divide the recurrence-weighted law by

\[ N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right). \]

Then

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

This is the compact form of the R2D law.

Expand the Local Asymmetry

Because

\[ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} - \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}, \]

the law becomes

\[ \boxed{ \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} - \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

The left side is read at the local closure boundary.

The right side is read through the enclosing occurrence.

The equality relates them without identifying them as the same local quantity.

What the Left Side Means

The left side asks:

How much enclosing-defined directional asymmetry remains around one completed compatible local closure?

Possibility contributes

\[ \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}. \]

Local productive retention contributes

\[ \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}. \]

Their difference is

\[ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}. \]

This is the local reading of the recursive relation.

What the Right Side Means

The right side asks:

How much constrained distinguishability is readable per enclosing recurrence, and how often does that recurrence occur relative to the local closure?

The constrained enclosing distinction is

\[ C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}. \]

Its recurrence relative to the local closure is

\[ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. \]

Together:

\[ C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. \]

This is the enclosing reading of the same recursive relation.

Why Recurrence Is Necessary

Suppose the local and enclosing boundaries recur one-for-one.

Then

\[ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}=1, \]

and the law reduces to

\[ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}. \]

But recursive boundaries need not recur one-for-one.

A local closure may recur many times for every enclosing recurrence.

Then

\[ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}<1. \]

The recurrence factor is therefore necessary to compare the two boundary-specific readings correctly.

The law does not equate one local event with one enclosing event.

It relates their recurrence-weighted structure.

A Simple Numerical Example

Suppose one local closure has

\[ \Delta A_{\mathcal C}=2. \]

Suppose that within the same comparison domain the local closure recurs

\[ 1000 \]

times while the enclosing occurrence recurs

\[ 100 \]

times.

Then

\[ \rho = \frac{100}{1000} = 0.1. \]

The R2D law requires

\[ 2 = C\lambda(0.1). \]

Therefore

\[ C\lambda=20. \]

The local boundary reads asymmetry \(2\) per local recurrence.

The enclosing boundary reads constrained distinguishability \(20\) per enclosing recurrence.

Because the enclosing occurrence recurs only one tenth as often, the recurrence-weighted totals agree:

\[ 2(1000)=20(100). \]

Thus:

\[ 2000=2000. \]

The two per-recurrence quantities are different.

Their recurrence-weighted recursive relation is the same.

The Law Does Not Say Local Retention Equals Enclosing Constraint

This distinction is important.

Local retention is

\[ \Delta R_n = C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} \lambda_n. \]

The enclosing side of the R2D law is

\[ C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1}. \]

These are not the same quantity.

In particular,

\[ \boxed{ \Delta R_n \neq C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1} } \]

in general.

They use different distinguishable intervals and different readings of constraint at adjacent boundaries.

Constraint and Retention →

Productive and Background-Associated Constraint

The total enclosing constraint can be decomposed as

\[ \boxed{ C_{n+1} = C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} + C_{n+1}^{\Phi}. } \]

The productive/output component

\[ C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} \]

is the component that enters local retention:

\[ \Delta R_n = C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} \lambda_n. \]

The background-associated component

\[ C_{n+1}^{\Phi} \]

does not enter local retention.

But both components contribute to the total enclosing constrained distinguishability read.

Therefore the R2D law becomes

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = \left( C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} + C_{n+1}^{\Phi} \right) \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

The Two Constraint Components Are Not Two Pathways

The decomposition

\[ C_{n+1} = C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} + C_{n+1}^{\Phi} \]

does not mean the enclosing occurrence splits into two separate transitions.

Nor does it mean two independent streams travel upward or downward.

Both terms belong to the same enclosing occurrence.

They distinguish two readable aspects of its constrained realization:

\[ \boxed{ C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} \lambda_{n+1} \longleftrightarrow \text{retained distinguishable output} } \]

and

\[ \boxed{ C_{n+1}^{\Phi} \lambda_{n+1} \longleftrightarrow \text{background-associated realization}. } \]

These are boundary-specific readings, not separate causal channels.

The R2D Law Is Not the Second Law

This distinction is especially important.

The R2D law relates

\[ \boxed{ \text{local recurrence-weighted asymmetry} } \]

to

\[ \boxed{ \text{enclosing recurrence-weighted constrained distinguishability}. } \]

The R2D Second Law addresses something else.

It relates local opposed-leg occurrence nonreciprocity to an enclosing background increment.

Schematically:

\[ \boxed{ \text{R²D law:} \qquad \text{local closure geometry} \longleftrightarrow \text{enclosing constrained geometry} } \]

whereas

\[ \boxed{ \text{Second Law:} \qquad \text{local occurrence nonreciprocity} \longleftrightarrow \text{enclosing background increment}. } \]

They are complementary postulates.

They are not two forms of the same equation.

The R²D Second Law →

Background-Associated Constraint Is Not the Background Increment

The R2D law contains

\[ C_{n+1}^{\Phi} \lambda_{n+1}. \]

The Second Law later contains

\[ \delta_{\mathcal C}\Phi_{n+1}. \]

Part I does not postulate

\[ C_{n+1}^{\Phi} \lambda_{n+1} = \delta_{\mathcal C}\Phi_{n+1}. \]

These quantities play different roles.

The first belongs to the decomposition of enclosing constrained distinguishability.

The second is the enclosing read of local occurrence-count nonreciprocity.

A later physical mapping may establish relationships between their physical reads in a specific domain.

That relationship is not primitive to Part I.

Why One Representative Peer Is Enough

An enclosing occurrence can have many compatible peer realizations:

\[ \chi_{n+1}^{(r)} \Longrightarrow \left\{ \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right\}_{\alpha\in I_n}. \]

Suppose peers \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are equivalent under the same enclosing compatibility relation.

Then either may serve as a representative local realization.

Applying the law to each gives

\[ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right) = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \chi_{n+1} \right), \]

and

\[ \Delta A_{n,\beta,\mathcal C} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\beta} \right) = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \chi_{n+1} \right). \]

Therefore,

\[ \boxed{ \frac{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} }{ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha} } = \frac{ \Delta A_{n,\beta,\mathcal C} }{ \rho_{n+1|n,\beta} } = C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}. } \]

Equivalent peers share the same enclosing recursive relation.

Representative Does Not Mean Average

The representative peer is not obtained by averaging all local peers.

Nor does one selected peer causally stand in for the contributions of the others.

It is representative because equivalent peers occupy the same compatibility class relative to the enclosing occurrence.

Thus:

\[ \boxed{ \text{representative peer} \neq \text{average peer}. } \]

If peer closures are not equivalent under the enclosing relation, their indices must remain explicit.

No averaging over nonequivalent peers is assumed.

The Law Is Not Bottom-Up Export

An earlier intuition might suggest that the local quantity

\[ \Delta A \]

is somehow exported upward and becomes

\[ C\lambda. \]

That is not the current R2D architecture.

The law does not describe transport of a substance between scales.

It does not say:

\[ \Delta A_n \longrightarrow C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}. \]

Instead, it says that the same recursively organized realization has two boundary-specific mathematical readings whose recurrence-weighted forms are equal.

Thus:

Nothing has to travel from the local closure to the enclosing occurrence.

The boundary changes.

The count meaning changes.

The invariant recursive relation remains.

The Law Is Not a Conservation Law of Substance

For the same reason, the R2D law should not be interpreted as conservation of a material quantity.

The equality

\[ \Delta A_n N_n = C_{n+1}\lambda_{n+1}N_{n+1} \]

does not imply that some substance has been physically transferred from the left side to the right side.

It states that two count representations of one recursively organized realization agree when recurrence is properly included.

The invariant is relational.

Three Statements Must Remain Separate

The recursive architecture can now be summarized by three equations.

Causality

\[ \boxed{ \chi_{n+1}^{(r)} \Longrightarrow \mathcal C_{n,\alpha}. } \]

This asks:

Which local realization is compatible with the enclosing occurrence?

Recursive carry

\[ \boxed{ \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \xmapsto{\kappa_n} \chi_{n+1}^{(r)}. } \]

This asks:

How does the completed local realization change count meaning when the boundary changes?

The R2D law

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

This asks:

How are the two boundary-specific realization geometries related after recurrence is accounted for?

These are three different questions.

They should not be collapsed into one arrow.

Why the Law Is Recursive

The law contains quantities from two adjacent boundaries simultaneously.

The local side contains

\[ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}. \]

The enclosing side contains

\[ C_{n+1}, \qquad \lambda_{n+1}. \]

The bridge between them is

\[ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. \]

Thus the law cannot be reduced to a relation confined entirely to one boundary.

Its primitive structure is intrinsically recursive:

\[ \boxed{ B_n \longleftrightarrow B_{n+1}. } \]

That is what distinguishes it from an ordinary within-scale state equation.

Why the Law Is Dimensionless

Every quantity in Part I remains a count relation.

\(\Delta A\) is a logarithmic count asymmetry.

\(C\) is a dimensionless constraint coordinate.

\(\lambda\) is a distinguishable count interval.

\(\rho\) is a ratio of recurrence counts.

Therefore,

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A = C\lambda\rho } \]

is defined before any physical units are assigned.

This is deliberate.

R2D first defines the recursive relation.

Later science can determine how a particular physical system reads each quantity.

Why the Mathematics Comes Before Units →

Physical Rate Can Come Later

The recurrence ratio

\[ \rho_{n+1|n} \]

is not a physical rate in Part I.

But a later domain-specific mapping may identify

\[ \rho_{n+1|n} \]

with a ratio of physical recurrence rates.

For example, if a physical system supplies valid mappings

\[ r_n \longleftrightarrow \text{local recurrence rate} \]

and

\[ r_{n+1} \longleftrightarrow \text{enclosing recurrence rate}, \]

then one may test whether

\[ \rho_{n+1|n} \longleftrightarrow \frac{r_{n+1}}{r_n}. \]

That is an empirical calibration.

It is not assumed by the primitive law.

Physical Energy Can Come Later

Likewise, a later thermodynamic mapping may assign an energetic scale to the dimensionless quantities.

Then the R2D relation may acquire familiar physical units on both sides.

But the physical mapping must preserve the primitive recursive structure.

The logical order remains

\[ \boxed{ \text{dimensionless recursive law} \rightarrow \text{physical calibration}. } \]

Not the reverse.

This is why one law can in principle be tested in very different physical domains.

What Would Test the Law

The R2D law is a postulate of the framework.

It is not a combinatorial identity that follows automatically from the definitions of boundary, multiplicity, or closure.

Its empirical burden is therefore substantial.

A physical realization must supply independent readings of:

\[ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C}, \]

\[ C_{n+1}, \]

\[ \lambda_{n+1}, \]

and

\[ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}, \]

and test whether

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

A successful mapping must also preserve the causal distinction:

the local closure realizes the enclosing relation;

it does not become its primitive cause.

What Would Falsify the Relation

Within a proposed physical mapping, the R2D law would fail if the independently identified boundary-specific quantities could not satisfy the recursive relation without arbitrary redefinition.

In particular, one should not rescue the law by silently redefining:

  • the count boundary;
  • the local closure;
  • the enclosing occurrence;
  • the distinguishable interval;
  • the constraint;
  • or the recurrence ratio

after the empirical comparison is made.

The mapping must be specified first.

The relation must then be tested.

This is central to the Run R²D program.

The Architecture in One View

The complete adjacent-boundary architecture is

\[ \boxed{ \begin{array}{ccccc} && \chi_{n+1}^{(r)} && \\[4pt] & \swarrow && & \\[-2pt] \text{causal compatibility} && \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} && \\[8pt] && \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \xmapsto{\kappa_n} \chi_{n+1}^{(r)} && \\[8pt] && \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha} && \end{array} } \]

The first relation defines local realization.

The second changes count meaning.

The third relates the recurrence-weighted geometry of the two readings.

Together they form the central recursive architecture of R2D.

Core Equations

Local closure asymmetry

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} - \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} } \]

Adjacent recurrence ratio

\[ \boxed{ \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha} = \frac{ N_{\mathcal D_R}(\chi_{n+1}) }{ N_{\mathcal D_R}(\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}) } } \]

Recurrence-weighted R2D law

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \mathcal C_{n,\alpha} \right) = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} N_{\mathcal D_R} \left( \chi_{n+1} \right) } \]

Compact R2D law

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha} } \]

Expanded form

\[ \boxed{ \Delta P_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} - \Delta R_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = C_{n+1} \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha} } \]

Total enclosing constraint

\[ \boxed{ C_{n+1} = C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} + C_{n+1}^{\Phi} } \]

Constraint-resolved form

\[ \boxed{ \Delta A_{n,\alpha,\mathcal C} = \left( C_{n+1}^{\mathrm{out}} + C_{n+1}^{\Phi} \right) \lambda_{n+1} \rho_{n+1|n,\alpha}. } \]

The Key Distinction

The R2D law does not say that local asymmetry is exported upward.

It does not say that a subordinate closure creates an enclosing occurrence.

And it does not say that the same physical state persists unchanged across the boundary.

It says something more specific:

One recursively organized realization has different readable forms at adjacent boundaries.

Those forms can recur at different counts.

When recurrence is included, their boundary-specific realization geometries satisfy one invariant relation.

That is the R2D law.

The next question is different:

What happens when the opposed legs of a completed local closure are realized with unequal occurrence-count efficiency?

That is the R2D Second Law.
